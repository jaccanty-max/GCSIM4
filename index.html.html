Analytical & Theoretical Expressions

Governing equations for Phase 3 open tubular gas chromatography hydrodynamics, partition thermodynamics, and Golay band broadening.

Primary Model Golay Equation for Open Tubular Capillary Columns (Plate Height $H$)

$$H = \frac{2 D_m}{u} + \frac{(1 + 6k + 11k^2) d_f^2}{24(1+k)^2 D_s} \cdot u$$

Where $D_m$ is the solute diffusion coefficient in mobile gas phase ($\text{cm}^2/\text{s}$), $D_s$ is the solute diffusion coefficient in stationary liquid phase ($\text{cm}^2/\text{s}$), $u$ is average linear gas velocity ($\text{cm/s}$), $d_f$ is stationary phase film thickness ($\text{cm}$), and $k$ is solute retention factor.

1 Hydrodynamics & Gas Velocity

$$u_m = \frac{F}{\pi r^2}$$
$$V_m = L \cdot \pi r^2, \quad t_m = \frac{L}{u_m}$$

$r = \frac{d_i}{2}$ is column radius in cm, $L$ is column length in cm ($L_{\text{cm}} = L_{\text{m}} \times 100$), $F$ is flow rate in $\text{cm}^3/\text{s}$ ($\text{mL/min} / 60$), $u_m$ is average linear velocity in $\text{cm/s}$, and $t_m$ is dead time in seconds.

2 Stationary Phase Volume ($V_s$) & Partition Thermodynamics

$$V_s = \pi \cdot L \cdot d_f \cdot (d_i - d_f)$$
$$\beta = \frac{V_m}{V_s}, \quad k = \frac{K}{\beta}$$

Where $d_i$ is column internal diameter in cm, $d_f$ is stationary phase film thickness in cm, $\beta$ is column phase ratio, and $V_s$ is stationary phase volume ($\text{cm}^3$).

3 Spatial Variance & Peak Broadening Equations

$$\sigma_x^2 = H \cdot L \implies \sigma_x = \sqrt{HL}$$
$$\sigma_t = \frac{\sigma_x}{u_m}$$
$$w_b = 4\sigma_t$$

Phase 4 Temperature-Programmed Elution & Numerical Integration

$$T(t) = T_0 + r \cdot t, \quad k(T) = \frac{1}{\beta}\exp\left(\frac{\Delta H_{\text{vap}}}{RT} - \frac{\Delta S_{\text{vap}}}{R}\right)$$
$$L = \int_0^{t_r} \frac{u_m}{1 + k(T(t))}\,dt \implies \sum \frac{u_m \Delta t}{L(1 + k(T(t)))} = 1$$
